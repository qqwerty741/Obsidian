Modern technology plays an important role in teenagers’ lives, as shown by the survey results from Zetland. The chart shows which devices are most popular among teenagers.  

The most commonly used device is the phone, chosen by 40% of teenagers. Phones are very convenient because they are small and can be used for many purposes, such as communication, social media, and entertainment. The second most popular device is the tablet, with 25% of teenagers using it. Tablets are also portable but have larger screens, which is useful for studying or watching videos. Laptops come next, with 23%. They are probably used for schoolwork or gaming. Desktop computers are less popular, with only 9%, and televisions are the least used, at just 3%.  

When comparing phones and tablets, phones are more popular because they are easier to carry. Similarly, laptops are preferred over desktop computers because they can be used anywhere.  

However, using technology too much can cause problems, like spending less time outdoors or becoming addicted to screens. To solve this, teenagers should take regular breaks and spend more time on physical activities.  

In conclusion, modern technology is very important for teenagers. It helps them study, communicate, and have fun. But it’s also important to use it wisely to stay healthy and balanced.
