$$\int{\dfrac{{x}^{3}+{x}^{2}-1}{\left({x-1}\right)^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}x}=\left|\begin{array}{lr}u=x-1\\x=u+1\\dx=du\end{array}\right|=\int{\dfrac{\left({u+1}\right)^{3}+\left({u+1}\right)^{2}-1}{{u}^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}u}=\int{\dfrac{\left({u+1}\right)^{3}}{{u}^{2}}+\dfrac{\left({u+1}\right)^{2}}{{u}^{2}}-\dfrac{1}{{u}^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}u}=\int{\dfrac{\left({u+1}\right)^{3}}{{u}^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}u}+\int{\dfrac{\left({u+1}\right)^{2}}{{u}^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}u}-\int{\dfrac{1}{{u}^{2}}}{\;\mathrm{d}u}=3\,\ln\left(\left|u\right|\right)+\dfrac{{u}^{3}+6\,{u}^{2}-2}{2\,u}+=2\,\ln\left(\left|u\right|\right)+\dfrac{{u}^{2}-1}{u}+\frac1u=$$
$$=5\,\ln\left(\left|u\right|\right)+\dfrac{{u}^{3}+8\,{u}^{2}-2}{2\,u}=\dfrac{{x}^{3}+5\,{x}^{2}-13\,x+5}{2\,\left(x-1\right)}+5\,\ln\left(\left|x-1\right|\right)+C$$
